## Featured Work

### Rise by Synchrony

**Financial wellness and credit-building experience for students**

Rise explores how financial products can help students build healthy credit habits without hiding the consequences of their decisions.

The experience combines a graduated credit card, credit-health dashboard, contextual guidance, rewards, educational resources, and an expressive companion named Pip.

**Focus:**
Product Strategy · UX Research · Interaction Design · Financial UX · Design Systems · AI Guidance

**[View Case Study →](https://emmanuella-turkson-portfolio.vercel.app/home)**

### MedEdge

**Clinical decision-support experience for healthcare professionals**

MedEdge explores how clinicians can identify critical patient-specific risks at the right moment without increasing cognitive overload or taking away clinical control.

The project focuses on transforming fragmented clinical information into a clearer decision-making experience.

**Focus:**
UX Research · Clinical Workflows · Information Architecture · AI UX · Interaction Design · High-Fidelity Prototyping

**[View Case Study →](https://emmanuella-turkson-portfolio.vercel.app/home)**

### Zoom Redesign

**Reimagining accessibility and usability within the video-meeting experience**

This redesign investigates accessibility challenges within virtual meetings and explores ways to make important controls, communication, and meeting information easier to understand and access.

**Focus:**
Accessibility · User Research · Competitive Analysis · Interaction Design · UI Design

## My Design Approach

```text
Understand
    ↓
Frame the right problem
    ↓
Explore
    ↓
Prototype
    ↓
Test + iterate
    ↓
Refine the system
    ↓
Ship the experience
```

I approach design as a decision-making process rather than a sequence of screens.

The goal is to understand **what matters, why it matters, and what the product should help someone do next.**

## Beyond Design

My background in **Computer Science, software engineering, machine learning, and AI** influences the way I approach product design.

It allows me to think beyond the interface and consider:

* how products are implemented;
* technical constraints;
* data and AI behavior;
* system architecture;
* edge cases;
* feasibility;
* collaboration between design and engineering.

I am especially interested in products where thoughtful UX can make complex technology easier to understand and use.
